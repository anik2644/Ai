"Ballot Box Battles: Controversies and Clashes Mar Global Elections"

Elaboration: Tensions surrounding elections have escalated worldwide, marked by controversies and clashes at polling stations. From accusations of voter suppression to violent confrontations over disputed results, electoral processes in various regions have been fraught with turmoil. Heightened polarization and distrust in political institutions have fueled these confrontations, underscoring the urgent need for transparent and inclusive electoral practices to uphold democratic principles and quell unrest.
