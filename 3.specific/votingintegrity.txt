 "Election Disruption: Cyber Threats Cast Shadow Over Voting Integrity"

Elaboration: With the rise of cyber threats targeting electoral systems, concerns about the integrity of voting processes have intensified. Recent reports reveal sophisticated hacking attempts aimed at disrupting voting infrastructure and manipulating ballot counts in several countries. As nations gear up for pivotal elections, authorities face mounting pressure to fortify cybersecurity measures and safeguard the sanctity of democratic processes against nefarious interference.
