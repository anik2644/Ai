Police have detained three people, including two women, for questioning after a transgender person was allegedly set on fire in Noakhali’s Senbagh Upazila. 

The victim was identified as Chumki, Officer-in-Charge (OC) Iqbal Hossain Patwari of Senbagh police confirmed the matter to Bangla Tribune.

According to police and locals, a newborn’s family in the neighbourhood wanted to hold a naming ceremony for the baby next Sunday.

Upon receiving the news, two transgender persons, including Chumki, of the area went to ask for money at Zahirul Haque Nayon’s house around 2pm on Thursday. 

At that time, the baby’s family gave them Tk200, but they demanded more money. At one stage of the altercation, Nayan's family members allegedly set Chumki on fire.

However, accused Bibi Rahima claimed that Chumki set herself on fire to intimidate the family as they had refused to pay Tk5,000. 

Abu Bakkar Siddique, a member of Ward No 2 of Kadra Union Parishad, said he went to the spot after learning about the incident. 

Citing the family members of the accused, he said that Chumki had taken fire from their stove and put it on herself.

The matter would be investigated, said police.
